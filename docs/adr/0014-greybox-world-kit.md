# 0014 — A readable greybox world kit (stand-in art style)

Status: accepted (2026-11-16). A detour inside M2 so the fast loop can be judged in a world that reads.

## Context

Plain primitives made the M2 region hard to judge: you could not tell woods from meadow at a glance, and
nothing said "overgrown Southeast homestead". No assets are being bought (ADR 0010) and the final art
direction is not chosen (ADR 0012). Travis supplied reference images (`ArtReference/`).

## Decisions

- **A stand-in style: flat-shaded low-poly in the references' palette.** It is meant to communicate place and
  mood, and to be replaced or polished later, not to be the final look.
- **Models are made in Blender by script** (`ArtSource/Greybox/region_kit.blend`, built through the Blender
  MCP connection) and exported as FBX to `Assets/Hearthline/Art/Greybox`: 5 trees, ground cover (bushes,
  fern, grass, wildflowers, rocks, kudzu mound and drape, fallen log), the cabin, fence, clothesline,
  woodpile, garden bed, rain barrel, guardrail bridge, overpass pieces, jersey barrier and rubble.
- **One palette, one material per colour.** The Blender export writes `palette.json`; `GreyboxKit` makes one
  URP material per entry (`HL_*`) and remaps every model to them, so a colour changes everywhere at once.
- **Ground is a painted colour map**, generated by the region builder from the same rules as the layout
  (meadow, dry patches, forest floor, rock on steep slopes, creek mud and gravel, cracked highway with a
  faded centre line, the trampled yard and path). No splat shader needed.
- **Trees and ground cover are drawn with GPU instancing** (`InstancedScatter`), culled in 40–60 m cells by
  distance from the main camera. Trees get invisible trunk colliders; ground cover has none. Rocks, logs,
  buildings and ruins are ordinary objects with mesh colliders.
- **Atmosphere** (`Atmosphere`): sun and moon colour and strength, trilight ambient, sky exposure and
  exponential fog follow the clock's hour; fog thickens in the deep woods and Thin places. A global
  post-processing volume adds ACES tonemapping, a warm grade, bloom and vignette. Every value is a curve,
  gradient or profile setting for Travis to tune; the ones set here are starting points.

## Consequences

- `M2_Region.unity` is large (about 8 MB) because instance matrices are stored in the scene. If it becomes
  a problem, move them into a ScriptableObject asset.
- Ground cover only draws near the main camera; screenshots from other cameras miss it.
- The mannequin still stands in for people; MPFB (CC0 humans in Blender) is the suggested route when
  characters are tackled.
