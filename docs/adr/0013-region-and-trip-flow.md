# 0013 — Greybox region and the trip flow in the world

Status: accepted (2026-11-16). M2 step 1.

## Decisions

- **Places are boxes.** `AreaVolume`s carry area tags from the vocabulary, a place name, a Thin level and
  whether they are the homestead. They have no colliders. `PlayerLocation` reads the ones around the player
  (smallest box names the place, tags are combined) and builds the simulation's `EventContext`, so events,
  the storyteller and mark origins ("at the fallen overpass") know where the party is. Distance from home is
  measured from the homestead centre against the region radius.
- **Leaving and coming back are walking.** Leaving the homestead box opens the outfit panel; setting out
  sends `SetOut`. Entering it on a trip sends `ReturnHome` and shows an arrival panel. Nothing about the
  trip is kept outside the simulation except the arrival panel's text.
- **The clock is presentation.** The simulation counts days; `TravelClock` runs the hours, turns the sun,
  and sends `AdvanceDays` at midnight or when the party makes camp (sleep to 6:00). It stops while a panel
  holds the player, and owes days the simulation refuses during an event. The hour is not saved: a load
  starts the day at 8:00. If time of day ever needs to persist, it moves into the simulation.
- **Panels pause the player through one interface.** `IPausesPlayer` (cheat panel, mirror, trip panels);
  `PlayerInputRelease` (renamed from `CheatPanelInputRelease`) frees the mouse while any of them wants it.
- **Scenes are generated by editor code.** Builders moved into the `Hearthline.Editor` assembly
  (`SceneBuildKit` for the shared player rig, ground, materials; one builder per scene) with menu items.

## Not done

- A new game starts with empty stores; starting supplies are for the homestead step.
- Travel speed does not yet slow when overloaded, and the party does not eat on the road (ADR 0008).
- Companions do not appear in the world.
