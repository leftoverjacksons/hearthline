# Idea pile

Things worth doing that are not scheduled yet. Not design decisions: when one is picked up, it gets a spec
(and an ADR or a `DESIGN.md` change if it alters the design).

## Gathering and hauling

- **Gathering animations.** Bend over and pick from a bush, kneel to gather herbs, stoop to lift deadfall,
  rummage through salvage. Today gathering is instant. (Travis, 2026-11-16, after first playtest.)
- **Salvage on a sled.** Heavy salvage goes on a makeshift sled dragged behind you on a tether: it slows
  you, and you can grab or drop the tether as needed (drop it to fight or sneak, pick it up again). Fits
  the design's "hauling home is gameplay" and the stable's later pack animals. (Travis, 2026-11-16.)
- **Pack that fills rather than swells.** Growing the pack by scaling it reads oddly. Swap between a few
  pack states (slack, full, bulging, items strapped on) instead.
